Mokshith Enterprises Solar Website

Core instruction

Build a premium solar-energy website for Mokshith Enterprises, using
the provided BlueWave reference screenshot as the primary visual and
structural reference.

The result should feel very close to the reference in: - overall section
rhythm - whitespace - typography scale - image-to-text balance -
alternating content sections - full-width photographic sections -
dark/light contrast - contact form presentation - minimal premium
aesthetic

Do not copy BlueWave branding, logo, wording, or exact assets.
Recreate the same quality and design language as an original Mokshith
Enterprises website.

Page structure

Follow this overall sequence:

Transparent/sticky navigation

Full-screen solar hero

Minimal anchor/category navigation below hero

Intro / Solutions heading

Residential Solar --- image + text

Community / Commercial Solar --- reversed image + text

Solar Development / Industrial Solar --- image + text

Full-width cinematic solar/environment section

"Why solar" / benefits section with three visual cards

Dark cinematic company mission section

Featured insights / news cards

Contact / quote section

Large dark navy footer

Hero

Use a large, realistic solar-panel landscape as the background.

The hero must resemble the reference's calm photographic composition
rather than a typical SaaS hero.

Headline direction:

Powering a brighter future with solar energy.

Supporting copy should be concise.

Primary CTA: Get a Free Quote

Secondary CTA: Explore Solar Solutions

Keep the hero text centered and elegant.

Solutions section

Use large editorial-style alternating layouts rather than a grid of
generic cards.

Residential Solar

Image on one side, text on the other.

Commercial Solar

Reverse the image/text arrangement.

Industrial Solar

Return to the original arrangement.

Each section should include: - small category label - strong heading -
short paragraph - 2--3 concise benefit points - understated Learn More
CTA

Cinematic image sections

Use full-width photography as visual breaks.

Recommended imagery: - solar farm - rooftop panels - clean-energy
landscape - blue sky / horizon - solar panels at golden hour

Use subtle overlays only where text requires contrast.

Benefits

Create a restrained three-column section inspired by the reference.

Suggested cards: - Solar for Your Home - Solar for Your Business - Solar
for a Sustainable Future

Each card should have a realistic image and short editorial copy.

Mission section

Use a dark, cinematic background.

Headline:

We make clean energy practical for a brighter tomorrow.

Keep this section atmospheric and premium, with a concise paragraph and
CTA.

Insights

Use three editorial cards.

Possible topics: - Understanding rooftop solar - How solar can reduce
electricity costs - The future of clean energy in India

Do not invent company claims, awards, certifications, customer counts,
MW installed, or testimonials.

Use placeholders wherever real Mokshith Enterprises information is
unavailable.

Contact

Recreate the reference's strong contact-section feeling:

full-width atmospheric background

centered section heading

large clean white form panel

two-column form on desktop

dark navy submit button

Fields: - Full Name - Phone Number - Email Address - City / Location -
Property Type - Monthly Electricity Bill - Message

CTA: Request a Free Consultation

Footer

Deep navy footer with: - Mokshith Enterprises branding - short company
description - Company links - Solar Solutions links - Contact details -
social links - legal links - copyright

Brand direction

Primary colors: - deep navy - clean white - solar blue - restrained
green accent

Do not make the website heavily green. The reference is primarily clean
white, blue and dark navy; preserve that visual character.

Typography: Use a modern premium sans-serif such as Inter or
Manrope.

Animation

Keep animation subtle and sophisticated: - hero fade/reveal - smooth
image reveal - scroll-based fade-up - slight image parallax -
understated button hover - project/image hover zoom - sticky navbar
transition

Avoid excessive particles, bouncing elements, flashy gradients, or
distracting effects.

Responsiveness

The mobile version must be intentionally redesigned rather than merely
shrinking desktop.

Requirements: - zero horizontal overflow - no fixed-width content -
alternating sections become stacked - forms become one column - cards
become comfortable single-column/scroll layouts - navigation becomes a
clean mobile menu - typography scales naturally - imagery remains
visually strong

Test at: 320px, 375px, 390px, 430px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px and 1920px.

Content integrity

Never invent factual company information.

Use editable placeholders such as: - XX+ Years -
XXX+ Installations - X MW+ until real data is supplied.

The final result should look like a premium solar-energy company
website, not a generic AI-generated template.
